## Information Femata handles

Depending on how you use Femata, the service handles:

- Account information such as name, email address, role, instrument, profile photo, and settings. Firebase Authentication handles login credentials; Femata does not receive your password in readable form.
- Practice and learning information such as sessions, dates, duration, notes, goals, streaks, pieces, performances, badges, progress, and league participation.
- Studio information such as assignments, submissions, lesson times and attendance, shared lesson notes, messages, teacher-entered student details, and invoice/payment records entered by a teacher. Femata does not collect payment card or bank details through the app.
- Recordings, assignment attachments, profile images, and other files that you choose to upload.
- Device and notification information, including push-notification tokens, device identifiers, platform, and the time-zone offset used for reminders.
- Technical information processed by hosting, authentication, and delivery providers, which may include IP address, browser/device details, and request logs. Confirm provider settings and retention before publication.
- Information stored on your device, such as sign-in persistence, preferences, and temporary app data.

Teachers may enter information about students. Teachers are responsible for having authority to provide that information and for giving students or parents any notices they need to provide.

## How information is used

Femata uses information to create and secure accounts; provide practice tracking, lessons, assignments, messages, recordings, sharing, leagues, and reminders; support teachers and students; maintain and improve the service; prevent abuse; and respond to support and privacy requests.

## Who can see information

A teacher can see information the student submits or shares with that teacher, plus the studio information needed to provide lessons. The app is designed to keep private practice notes and unsubmitted recordings private from the teacher. Verify these statements against the current access rules before launch.

League information is shown according to the user's league and profile settings. A user-created share link is public to anyone who obtains the link. It can be forwarded, copied, previewed by messaging/social services, or cached; removing the link from Femata may not remove copies or cached previews elsewhere.

## Service providers and other disclosures

Femata uses Firebase for authentication, database, file storage, and related services; Vercel for hosting and server functions; Apple Push Notification service for Apple notifications; and web-push delivery providers including Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Mozilla, Apple, and Windows Push Notification Services where supported. Public share pages load Google Fonts. Confirm the current configured providers, their roles, contracts, and processing locations before publishing.

Femata does not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising in the reviewed app build. Information may also be disclosed when you choose to share it, when needed to operate the service through these providers, to respond to legal requirements, or to protect users and the service.

## Children

Femata currently intends to accept new accounts only from people aged 13 or older. Children under 13 must not create an account. If Femata learns it collected personal information from a child under 13, contact **help@femataus.com** so the account and information can be reviewed and removed. The signup screen currently uses self-attestation; it is not a verified age check. Because Femata's marketing refers to young students, counsel must assess whether the service is directed to children and whether COPPA obligations apply despite the age restriction. Do not launch until this is resolved.

Users under 18 should have a parent or guardian review the applicable terms and provide any permission required where they live. A parent-request and consent process is not currently implemented; decide and build it before accepting minors if needed.

## Retention, access, export, and deletion

Femata keeps account and service information while an account is active and for as long afterward as needed for service operation, safety, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Specific retention periods, backup deletion timing, and vendor retention must be confirmed before publication.

Users can download a JSON export from Privacy & Security. The current export includes selected account, profile, practice, piece, performance, recording, assignment, lesson-slot, and summary records; it does not currently include every message, studio record, invoice, notification record, or the contents of uploaded media files. The in-app description must not call it “everything.”

To request access, correction, or account/data deletion, email **help@femataus.com** from the account email where possible. Femata will verify the request, explain any information it must retain, and confirm what action it takes. Set and publish request-response and deletion timelines before launch. A teacher may have separate legal recordkeeping duties for invoices or studio records.

## Tracking and browser signals

The reviewed app build does not intentionally use advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral advertising. Public share pages load a Google Fonts resource. The site does not change its practices in response to a browser “Do Not Track” signal; no such signal overrides the choices described here. Confirm the marketing site and all analytics, cookies, SDKs, and logs before launch.

## Security and changes

Femata uses access controls and service-provider security features, but no online service can promise that information is always secure. Femata may update this notice; material changes should be communicated through the service or another appropriate channel. The effective date will be updated when a revision takes effect.

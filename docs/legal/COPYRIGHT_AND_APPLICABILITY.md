# Femata launch legal work — copyright and applicability

## Copyright handling: pre-launch checklist

Femata stores user-uploaded recordings, assignment files, photos, and other material. Before public launch:

- Decide whether to seek the hosting safe harbor under 17 U.S.C. § 512.
- If so, appoint a DMCA designated agent, register the agent with the U.S. Copyright Office, publish the agent's required contact information, and renew the registration on time.
- Adopt and reasonably implement a repeat-infringer policy.
- Create a notice-and-takedown process, a counter-notice process, a way to identify reported content, and a record of actions taken.
- Have counsel review the copyright license in the Terms and the handling of copyrighted music, scores, and recordings.

The email help@femataus.com can receive initial reports, but it is not a registered DMCA agent. A public mailing address and legal identity are still needed to complete the agent setup. Copyright Office guidance: https://www.copyright.gov/512/.

## Preliminary U.S. applicability assessment

This is a screening memo, not a legal opinion. It assumes an individual operator, a U.S.-only launch, no published users yet, no advertising or sale of personal information in the reviewed app, and the current 13+ signup policy.

### COPPA — unresolved launch blocker

The signup screen now blocks users who say they are under 13. That does not by itself prove COPPA is inapplicable. Femata's public messaging describes young students, and the product collects names, emails, persistent account identifiers, recordings, profile images, and education/practice data. Have counsel assess whether the service is directed to children under 13 or has actual knowledge of under-13 users. If COPPA applies, stop collection until compliant notice, verifiable parental consent, parent rights, security, and retention/deletion practices are in place. FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions.

### California CalOPPA — likely policy obligation if California users are served

If the commercial site or online service collects personal information from California residents, a conspicuous and accurate privacy policy is generally required. Femata collects account and profile information, so publish a final policy that matches both the app and marketing site. California DOJ guidance: https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/facts/online-privacy/privacy-policy.

### California CCPA/CPRA — applicability not yet determined

The CPPA's currently published revenue threshold is over $26.625 million annual gross revenue. Other tests include buying, selling, or sharing personal information of 100,000 or more consumers/households, or deriving 50% or more of annual revenue from selling/sharing it. Femata has no published users yet, but the operator must confirm revenue and business relationships and re-check when the service grows. If covered, implement required notices, rights-request handling, opt-out and other controls. CPPA guide: https://cppa.ca.gov/pdf/business_comply.

### Other U.S. obligations to revisit

- Confirm applicable state privacy laws as user volume and target states become known.
- If schools or districts adopt Femata, review education-record contracts and FERPA responsibilities; educational use alone does not automatically make FERPA apply.
- If accepting users aged 13–17, decide what parental permission and age-appropriate safeguards are needed for the chosen states and app stores.
- Reassess all of the above before adding ads, analytics, payments, new vendors, or international markets.

## Facts still needed before publication

- Final launch states/countries and minimum-age/minor permission model.
- Revenue, consumer/household counts, and any data sale/share revenue when the service begins operating.
- Firebase, Vercel, push-provider, and Google Fonts settings, data regions, contracts, and retention periods.
- The actual retention period for accounts, media, backups, server logs, and deletion requests.
- Whether the separate marketing website uses cookies, analytics, or other third parties not present in this app checkout.
